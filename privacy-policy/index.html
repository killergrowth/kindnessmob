Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

The Kindness Mob (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it when you visit this website or interact with our community.

Information We Collect

When you submit a nomination or contact form, we may collect your name, email address, and the content of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry or process your nomination. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand how many people visit and what pages they view. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics. We do not use advertising cookies.

Third-Party Links

Our website links to social platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Discord) and donation platforms (Buy Me a Coffee). Each of these has its own privacy policy and we are not responsible for their practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page or via Discord.
