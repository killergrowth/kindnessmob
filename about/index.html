Our Story

How a moment of frustration became a daily movement of kindness.

Founded January 1, 2023

Hi, I’m Tyler.

Welcome to The Kindness Mob.

This project started on January 1st, 2023 — after I hit a critical moment of sadness while scrolling online. I’d watched how easy it was for people to jump on the bandwagon of hate if they saw other mean comments. And I started wondering…

What if we had a kindness bandwagon instead?

The music on our wagon is better. And we have snacks, too.

What We Do

We Raid with Kindness

Every day, we select one nominated creator and flood their comment section with hundreds of genuine, encouraging messages. We never mention the Kindness Mob — the kindness feels 100% organic because it is.

We’re Powered by Volunteers

No paid employees. No corporate backing. Just a community of people who believe a small act of coordinated kindness can change someone’s day — and do it often enough, change the world.

Our Mission: Kindness for Everyone, Always

We believe the internet doesn’t have to be a place of outrage and pile-ons. Our mission is to prove that kindness is just as contagious as cruelty — and a lot more fun to spread.
