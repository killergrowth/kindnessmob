Volunteer with the Mob

This whole thing runs on people who care. Be one of them.

How to Get Involved

The Kindness Mob is 100% volunteer-run. There’s no payroll, no office, no budget meetings. Just a community of people who believe in what we’re doing and show up every day to prove it.

1

Join the Daily Mob

The easiest way to volunteer is to just show up. Join our Discord, watch for the daily announcement, head to the creator’s profile, and leave a genuine kind comment. That’s volunteering.

2

Help Review Nominations

We get more nominations than we can handle. If you want to help vet and organize incoming nominations so the right people get picked, reach out through our Discord.

3

Help Us Grow

Tell your friends. Post about mobs you’re participating in (without revealing the target). Share what we do. The bigger the mob, the bigger the impact.
