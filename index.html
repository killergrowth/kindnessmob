How It Works

Three Steps to Spreading Kindness

You don’t need to sign up, pay a thing, or even mention us. Just show up and be kind.

1

Nominate

Know a creator who deserves some love? Nominate them on any platform — TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube.

2

We Pick a Mob Target

Every day we select one nomination. The only rule: we never mention the Kindness Mob so the comments feel 100% organic.

3

Join the Mob

Head to the creator’s latest post and drop a genuine, kind comment. That’s it. Watch someone’s day completely change.

Our Story

Started by Someone Who Got Tired of the Hate

On January 1st, 2023, Tyler reached a critical moment. He’d spent too much time watching people pile on with mean comments — and he started wondering if a kindness bandwagon could work just as well.

Spoiler: it does. And the music on our wagon is better. We also have snacks.

The Kindness Mob is run entirely by volunteers and fueled by the belief that one small act of coordinated kindness can have an enormous ripple effect.
