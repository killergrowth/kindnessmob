Our Story

Started by Someone Who Got Tired of the Hate

On January 1st, 2023, Tyler reached a critical moment. He’d spent too much time watching people pile on with mean comments — and he started wondering if a kindness bandwagon could work just as well.

Spoiler: It does. And the music on our wagon is better. We also have snacks.

The Kindness Mob is run entirely by volunteers and fueled by the belief that one small act of coordinated kindness can have an enormous ripple effect.

How It Works

5 Ws and an H

Everything you’re curious about, at a glance.

Who

Everyone, this one’s easy.

And we mean that in both directions. Anyone can join the Mob — no application, no experience, no minimum follower count. And anyone can be nominated to receive one. We don’t vet people to decide if they’ve earned kindness first. That’s the whole idea.

What

Kind comments.

Real ones. When a creator gets nominated, hundreds of us show up in their comment section and say something genuinely nice. Not spam, not a script. Just a wall of encouragement big enough to change what that comment section feels like.

Where

Comment sections.

Mostly on TikTok, right where the negativity usually lives. You’ll find each day’s mob posted on our page, with a link straight to the video. Tap, comment, done.

When

Every weekday and sometimes on weekends.

Nominations come in around the clock, we read them daily, and most days we pick someone and go. It takes about thirty seconds to participate, which is less time than it takes to argue with a stranger online.

Why

Everyone deserves it.

Also because negativity is organized and kindness usually isn’t. One mean comment makes the next one easier to type. We just run that in reverse: when the first five hundred comments are kind, the mean ones stop showing up.

How

Authentically.

Watch the video. Find something you actually like. Say that. No copy-paste comments, no bots, no faking it. Seven hundred people noticing something real beats ten thousand saying the same thing.
